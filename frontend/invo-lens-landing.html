step · 01

Automatic extraction

Upload a scanned or photographed invoice. Azure AI Content Understanding reads vendor, invoice number, date, line items, and total — no manual typing.

step · 02

Confidence, per field

Every extracted value carries a confidence score. Nothing is treated as certain just because it was extracted.

step · 03

Human-gated review

Low-confidence fields are flagged, not auto-approved. A person makes the final call — approve or flag — and it's logged.

How review actually works

Nothing is approved just because it was extracted.

→ Document received via upload
→ Sent to Azure AI Content Understanding
→ Fields extracted: vendor, number, date, total, items
✓ Vendor, number, date: high confidence
⚠ Total: confidence below threshold
→ Status set to "Needs review", not auto-approved
→ Awaiting human: Approve or Flag
Needs review

One field's confidence didn't clear the bar — so the whole invoice waits for a person, instead of shipping through on four good fields and one bad guess.

This is the actual logic running in the backend, not a mockup: confidence is checked per field, the invoice status is set automatically, and every approve/flag action is written to that invoice's own activity log.
